## Description
ETC1 RGB, no alpha, 4 bpp. Widely supported on older Android
devices and OpenGL ES 2.0+ GPUs. Quality is acceptable for
diffuse color but it's been superseded - prefer Etc2 or Astc
on newer hardware!
